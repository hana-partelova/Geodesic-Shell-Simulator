Geodesic Shell
Schwarzschild spacetime simulator
Spacetime Geometry
r_s = 2GM/c² — Schwarzschild radius (event horizon). Nothing with r < r_s escapes. Shell at R > r_s forms the boundary between the outer Schwarzschild and inner spacetime. M_shell is the shell's own gravitational mass. Interior: flat (Minkowski — shell theorem states a spherical shell creates no interior gravitational field) or uniform-density mass M_in.
Massive Particle (Timelike)
E ≥ 1 = particle has enough energy to escape (unbound). E < 1 = bound orbit (trapped in potential well). L = angular momentum; larger L → stronger centrifugal barrier (larger periapsis). ISCO = 3r_s is the innermost stable circular orbit. Use ⊙ Snap to auto-compute the correct L and E for a circular orbit at r₀.
Photon (Null Geodesic)
b_crit = (3√3/2)·r_s is the critical impact parameter. Photons with b < b_crit are captured by the black hole. Photons near b ≈ b_crit orbit on the photon sphere (1.5·r_s). Larger b → photon deflects but escapes (gravitational lensing). Lensing bundle fires N photons spread ±Δb around b — shows gravitational lensing at different impact parameters.

Numerical Integration (RK4)
Metóda Runge-Kutta 4. rádu (RK4) — najpoužívanejší numerický integrátor pre ODE. Chyba je úmerná dτ⁴. Menšie dτ → vyššia accuracy at the cost of computation time. ΔE/E is the relative energy conservation residual — a quality indicator (ideally < 10⁻⁵).
