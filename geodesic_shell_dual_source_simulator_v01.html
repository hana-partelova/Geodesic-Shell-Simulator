Geodesic Shell — Dual
Schwarzschild spacetime · two photon sources
Spacetime Geometry
r_s = 2GM/c² — Schwarzschild radius. Shell at R > r_s separates outer Schwarzschild from inner spacetime (flat Minkowski or uniform mass). Both sources orbit outside.
Source 1 — Massive particle (timelike)
E ≥ 1 = unbound. L = angular momentum. ISCO = 3r_s. Use ⊙ Snap for circular orbit.
Source 1 — Photon bundle (null geodesic)
b_crit = (3√3/2)·r_s. Photons b < b_crit are captured. Bundle fires N photons around b±Δb.
Source 2 — Enable / configure
Second photon source placed at (r₀₂, φ₀₂) in the same equatorial plane. Both sources are fired simultaneously at τ=0. The separation Δφ or Δr between sources lets you study wavefront meeting loci, caustic offsets, and differential Shapiro delay.
